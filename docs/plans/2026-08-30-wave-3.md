### U1 — the interrupt window

`crates/harness-app-server/src/lib.rs:321-340` responds to `turn/start` and notifies `turn/started`
inside a borrow of the writer, and installs the `TurnControl` in `self.active` only afterwards
(verified at `ee14baf`). An interrupt decoded in that window finds `active == None`, is acknowledged,
and cancels nothing.

The story carries **measured** evidence, not a reading: 21 red of 50 suite runs under
oversubscription, and 5/5 deterministic failure with a 200 ms sleep injected into the window. It also
carries the diff for two test cases **that have never been run**. The implementor applies them first
and finds out — an unrun test is a starting point, not evidence.

`contracts/cli/b10x-harness/`. Invariant 13 forbids editing a released one, so this cuts
`2026-08-30.2`.
