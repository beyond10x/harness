## What the scoper settled and what it did not (2026-10-20, medium confidence)

Settled, cited:

- The adapter is a crate of its own, below `harness-substrate` and `harness-cli`
  (`ConfinedOperations` implements the `Operations` trait at `crates/harness-substrate/src/tools.rs:28,152`).
- `crates/harness-toolchain` deletes outright: `b10x-tool-toolchain` 0.1.0 is the same code with a
  doc paragraph, `pub mod contract;` and a `plan` fallback added; the five builtin YAMLs are
  byte-identical.
- `crates/harness-tools` does not delete outright: `catalogue.rs`, `flat.rs`, `verbs.rs`,
  `scope.rs`, `toolchain.rs`, `operations.rs`, `tests.rs` and `local.rs`'s `grep`/`exec`/`drain`
  stay in harness (tools' provenance table says so); the reader and writer halves of `local.rs`
  are what `b10x-tool-fs` and `b10x-tool-code-editor` replace.
- Name mapping: `file_read → fs.read`, `dir_list → fs.list`, `find → fs.glob`,
  `file_write → code.write`, `file_edit → code.edit`; `search` and `run` have no tools operation in
  0.1.0 (T3) and stay local. `Entry.operation` (the recorded operation name, read back by
  metaharness) is a third column and stays as it is.
- The CLI contract (`contracts/cli/b10x-harness/2026-09-18/argv.json`) names no tool; the
  acceptance's "contract still lists every tool name" is met by argv staying byte-identical, and
  the tool-name list lives in `catalogue.rs:931-940` and `README.md:238-246`.

Not settled (the brief must decide or the implementor must report):

- The new crate's name (invariant 6 forbids `common`, `shared`, `utils`, `misc`, `helpers`).
- `tool_fs` adds `sha256` to every `fs.read` reply; `ConfinedOperations::file_read` answers none,
  so the substrate conformance suite's equality check breaks unless the adapter strips the field
  or the confined reply gains one.
- Behavioural drift between `local.rs` and the tools crates (overlapping-match counting, trailing
  `/`, `limit: 0`, bound in bytes): which of the 1,240 lines of `harness-tools/src/tests.rs` and
  the 34 conformance cases move or change was not checked.
